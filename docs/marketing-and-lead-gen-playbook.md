## 1. The funnel

Every channel below exists to do one thing: get a homeowner to the quote-request form on
`definitivestructuresllc.com`. Don't scatter effort across five platforms with five different
calls-to-action — the site's form is the single conversion point, everything else is a signpost
pointing at it.

## 2. Google Business Profile — the single biggest lever

This is the highest-leverage single thing to set up before any paid marketing:

- **Claim and completely fill out the profile.** Name/address/phone must match exactly across every
  place they appear online (site, GBP, any directory listing) — inconsistency actively hurts local
  ranking.
- **Categories**: pick the most specific accurate primary category, then add secondary categories
  that capture how people actually search (e.g., a specific "carport" or "outdoor structure" style
  category alongside general contractor).
- **Service area business setup**: since jobs happen at the customer's property, not a storefront,
  configure this as a service-area business and define the OKC metro cities we actually cover —
  don't list a home address publicly if the business doesn't need one.
- **Photos**: fresh jobsite photos, the truck in real neighborhoods, completed builds. Google's
  2026 update weighted engagement and photo/review recency more heavily than a static, unchanged
  listing.
- **Weekly posts**: doesn't need to be elaborate — a finished job photo with a one-line caption
  counts, and consistency matters more than production value.
- **Q&A section**: seed it ourselves with the questions people actually ask ("do I need a permit?",
  "how long does installation take?") instead of waiting for someone else to ask first.

## 3. Local SEO basics

- Make sure the site itself (already built on this repo) has clear, crawlable service-area content
  — city names in real sentences, not just a hidden list.
- List the business consistently (same NAP — name/address/phone) on any directory that's worth
  being on, rather than a scattershot of mismatched listings.
- Local SEO compounds slowly — the 2026 guidance across sources agrees on this: consistency beats
  speed. Don't judge it after two weeks.

## 4. Community channels

- **Nextdoor**: strong fit for this trade — neighbors trust neighbor recommendations for home
  projects specifically. Worth a business page and occasional posts showing completed local jobs.
- **Facebook local groups** (city-specific buy/sell/recommend groups): low-cost, high-relevance for
  home-improvement recommendation threads.
- **Yard signs at completed job sites** (with the homeowner's permission): free, hyper-local, and
  puts the business in front of exactly the kind of household likely to want the same thing.

## 5. The portfolio — our best marketing asset

Before/after photos are the single best asset a small carport builder has, and it costs nothing but
discipline to capture:

- Photograph every job: site before, mid-build, and finished — in good light, without job-site
  clutter in frame.
- Use these across every channel above: GBP posts, Nextdoor, Facebook — the same handful of great
  photos do double and triple duty.
- Prioritize the **simplest lean-to jobs** (per `finding-cheapest-kits-simplest-jobs.md`) early on
  specifically because they're fast to complete and fast to photograph — a full portfolio in weeks,
  not months.

## 6. Reviews

- Ask at the moment of completion, in person or by a same-day text/email — response rates drop fast
  once a customer walks away.
- **Reply to every review**, positive or negative — recency and engagement now matter more to
  ranking than raw review count.
- A steady trickle of new reviews beats a burst of many at once, then silence.

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Google Business Profile claimed, fully filled out, set up as a service-area business
- [ ] NAP (name/address/phone) identical across site, GBP, and any directory listing
- [ ] First batch of jobsite/portfolio photos taken and posted
- [ ] Nextdoor business page created
- [ ] Relevant local Facebook groups identified and joined
- [ ] Review-request message drafted for use immediately after each completed job
- [ ] Q&A section on GBP seeded with our own common questions (permits, timeline, materials)
- [ ] Confirmed every channel's link/CTA points back to the site's quote-request form
